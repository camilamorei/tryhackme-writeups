# Defensive Security Trends

## Overview

This room explores emerging trends in defensive cybersecurity and how modern threats are changing the way Security Operations Center (SOC) teams operate. It covers faster and more complex attacks, the abuse of valid accounts, supply chain compromises, and the impact of artificial intelligence on cybersecurity.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand how cyberattacks are becoming faster and more complex.
- Learn how adversaries obtain and abuse valid accounts.
- Understand supply chain attacks from a SOC perspective.
- Explore how AI affects both attackers and defenders.
- Identify strategies SOC teams can use to improve detection and response.

## Topics Covered

- Attack speed and automation
- Advanced attack techniques and evasion
- Network perimeter challenges
- Infostealers and stolen credentials
- Initial Access Brokers (IABs)
- Supply chain attacks
- Vercel supply chain incident
- AI-assisted security operations
- AI-powered cyberattacks
- MITRE ATT&CK and Cyber Kill Chain

## Key Takeaways

- Modern attacks can progress from initial access to ransomware deployment in a very short time.
- SOC teams should automate repetitive triage tasks and prioritize rapid containment.
- Attackers increasingly abuse legitimate tools and services to avoid detection.
- Infostealers can steal credentials, browser sessions, access tokens, and other authentication materials.
- Initial Access Brokers sell compromised access to organizations, often to ransomware groups.
- Supply chain attacks compromise trusted software, services, or dependencies to reach their targets.
- Strong visibility, least privilege, EDR, and effective detection coverage help reduce the impact of attacks.
- AI can assist with investigation, enrichment, deobfuscation, and report writing, but analysts must remain responsible for critical decisions.
- MITRE ATT&CK and the Cyber Kill Chain remain relevant even as attacks become more sophisticated.

## Conclusion

Defensive Security Trends highlights the importance of adapting SOC operations to an evolving threat landscape. Faster attacks, stolen credentials, supply chain compromises, and AI-powered threats require continuous improvement in detection, automation, and incident response. Security analysts must stay informed, understand attacker behavior, and use technology responsibly to protect organizations.
