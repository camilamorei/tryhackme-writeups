
# Training Impact on Teams

## Overview

This room explores the importance of cybersecurity training for individuals and organisations. It explains how training improves team productivity, helps junior employees develop skills, and provides financial benefits to companies.

## Task 1: Understanding the Impact of Cyber Security Training

Cybersecurity training helps employees develop and maintain the skills needed to respond to security incidents and use relevant tools effectively.

Training provides several benefits:

- Improves team productivity and preparedness.
- Helps junior employees develop skills more efficiently.
- Reduces the time senior employees spend repeating basic training.
- Establishes a common baseline for evaluating skills.
- Encourages teamwork through practical exercises and Capture the Flag (CTF) challenges.
- Reduces the likelihood and impact of security incidents.

### Answer

The most efficient way to ramp up the skills of a junior hire is through training.

## Task 3: Write a Cyber Security Training Investment Proposal

Training can provide measurable financial benefits by increasing employee productivity.

### Scenario

- Number of employees: 20
- Annual cost per employee: $50,000
- Productivity increase: 4%
- Training cost per employee: $500

### Calculating Productivity Savings

The savings are calculated by multiplying the number of employees by the annual cost per employee and the productivity improvement.

Savings = 20 × $50,000 × 0.04

Savings = $40,000

Answer: `$40,000`

### Calculating the Training Cost

Total training cost = 20 × $500

Total training cost = $10,000

### Calculating Return on Investment

ROI is calculated by dividing the financial gain by the training investment and multiplying by 100.

ROI = ($40,000 / $10,000) × 100

ROI = 400%

Answer: `400%`

This calculation follows the room's simplified method, which compares productivity savings with training costs.

## Task 4: Vendor Selection

Selecting a cybersecurity training provider requires evaluating the organisation's needs and the quality of the training offered.

Important considerations include:

- The experience, roles, and training requirements of employees.
- The vendor's experience with similar organisations.
- The breadth, depth, and quality of the training content.
- Opportunities to learn and practise on the same platform.
- Integration with existing systems.
- Training costs compared with the expected productivity benefits.

The best provider should offer training that matches the team's skill levels, responsibilities, and security objectives.

## Task 5: Conclusion

Cybersecurity training is an important investment in an organisation's security capabilities. It improves employee productivity, prepares teams to handle security incidents, and helps junior employees develop their skills.

A structured training programme also establishes a consistent baseline for measuring employee knowledge and identifying areas for improvement.

Financial calculations can help organisations justify training investments by comparing productivity gains with training costs. Selecting the right provider ensures that training meets the organisation's requirements and delivers practical value.

## Key Terms

- Cybersecurity Training: Education and practical exercises that develop security skills.
- Capture the Flag (CTF): A cybersecurity challenge used to practise technical skills.
- Content Studio: TryHackMe's feature for creating and customising organisational training content.
- Single Sign-On (SSO): Authentication method that allows access to multiple services through one sign-in.
- API: An interface that allows software applications to communicate.
- Productivity Savings: Financial value associated with improvements in employee productivity.
- Return on Investment (ROI): A measure used to compare financial gains with investment costs.
